## work-life balance

Long time ago, when i browse the red book's posts where is filled of well-groommed socialites, i encountered an abbreviation "WLB", 
which refers to "Work - Life - Balance".

As a totally student, i've never truly entered society and experienced working. I have no idea about work at all. 
Sometimes when i browse the posts about someone complaining about their job and work, i totally scoff at this ("对此完全嗤之以鼻" in chinese).
as at that time, i was working on a cross-functional project which was totally trash.
I couldn't get any money, the mentor knew nothing about technical details, and a lot of time was spent on pointless communcation.

I don't understand why? As a master student, though i'll go and get a job in the end, but at least i should do something meaningful, 
or what's the difference between master and undergraduate, espeacially with the help of AI agents.

**Are you saying that the purpose of master is just to do projects to make money for the professor?!**

As a result, i was very painful, slapdash results weekly (because there're seniors also working on the project), and regret being the master instead of working straightly. 

However now, i change my mind.

Start from the beginning, every once in a while, my attitude will totally change.


**Stage 1 - undergraduate Grade 3~4: **

I was very very afraid of working, and always worried about not getting a quota of guaranteed graduate school spot, because i just got high gpa, without any competitions or research paper bonus. I often had trouble sleeping, and need melatonin to fall asleep.

My mindset was really off too, jealous about other's results, worried about other's overall ranking higher than me, envious of others being able to better schools PHD or master, and finally wonder what could happen if i seized some opportunities. Over and over again.


**Stage 2 - undergraduate Grade 4 - joing the group in advance: **

At that time, i came into contact with the most disgusting job of master student - **cross-functional projects** ("横向" in chinese). I think every chinese master or PHD students understand how i feel. 99% of cross-functional projects are totally meaningless, unless your mentor is a industry expert and has a wide circle of connection with enterprises, and you can do something high-end. 

Otherwise, i could't imagine how come an enterprise will find a college professor to complete the requirement instead of looking for a professional company. Could it be that they don't know that it's always the students who end up doing this work?! As a result, most of the work is useless attempt, with a lot of time wasting on communication with the mentor, the company and even the hardware seller.

Am i the research newcomer or a coolie?

And sometimes the mentor will PUA: through doing cross-functional projects, you could get in touch with the real-world needs, and accumulate work experience.

**Nonesense**

**Stage 3 - Master Grade 1 - learning at a high-end startup: **

Getting rid of the cross-functional projects, i thought that my easygoing life would finally come. A new event has come up.

Since that my research focus is quite high-end - embodied intelligent, at the same time, it just so happened that one of my PHD senior started a company in a related field. My advisor wants me to go to the company to learn.

So i become part of corporate slave when i don't have classes.

The biggest feeling is that commuting really wears down your mood. Everytime i go to the company, i'll spend averagely 1 ~ 1.5h one way. Although less than a lot of people, i really feel that commuting makes me feel pretty annoyed.

Another feeling is that because i lack work experience, i'm timid and hesitant, afraid of messing things up. So i also appreciate having this opportunity to access to the true workplace atmosphere

And the most important thing is that, i'm really not suitable for high-end algorithm or development department. too much pressure.

My dream job is few tasks and close to home. Since the job-iron-triangle couldn't satisfy at the same time, i'm willing to sacrifise the money and improve the quality of life, just like the title of the blog "work-life-balance".


That's all for this blog. I need to take a shower, a little bit relax, and waiting for the training of the company tomorrow where 2 ~ 2.5h public transportation one way away from my home or school.
And that's why i'm at the hotel now. fk it!
