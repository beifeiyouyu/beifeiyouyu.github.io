## Criticism of group assignment

There's no doubt that Group Assignment is a big trash, and the more members the worse. As long as you choose the subtask like making ppt, or some paperwork fast, you'll enjoy the most relaxing Group assignment, especially with the help of ai nowadays.

Besides, if you're unfortunately assigned some important subtasks, as long as you're shameless enough (refer to “足够不要脸”) and hide， you can even do nothing at all. And that's the true essence of Group Assignment——

"The more anxious a person is, the more work he'll do."

fk it
{: .text-note}

I have to admit that most coursework is total trash, just using ai to finish it perfunctorily. But, since you have accepted your subtask, you need to finish it, which represents a sense of responsibility, let alone your work result is perhaps the preconditions of the others.

I can't understand why they're so relax. Even though i'm not care about the final grades of the course, at least you have to give in a complete course work, no matter about the results.
